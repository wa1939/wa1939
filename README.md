# Waleed Alhamed

This is my play area.

I build things that solve my own problems — and every once in a while, they end up solving someone else's too. Some builds are small tools, some turn into full products, and some never make it to the finish line. All of that is the point.

Off the playground: People Analytics, AI & Research @ ELM · Co-founder & CPO @ Neuravisor · MBA @ UIUC

## Currently building

- **[WorkOps](https://github.com/wa1939/WorkOps)** — self-hosted task, project, and workload management for departments: capacity planning, time tracking, leave, PMO views, and optional bring-your-own AI.
- **[Athar](https://github.com/wa1939/athar)** (أثر) — local-first Android personal finance app that reads bank SMS and auto-categorizes spending against a Saudi merchant dictionary. No cloud, no ads, no telemetry.
- **[termfolio](https://github.com/wa1939/termfolio)** — terminal-themed portfolio and blog that syncs directly with an Obsidian vault. No database required.
